# Your account

## Reset a forgotten password
Page: /forgot-password
For: all
Keywords: password, forgot, forgotten, reset, change password, cannot sign in, can't log in, locked out, login, sign in

On the sign-in page choose Forgot?, enter the email you use for TenantVoice
and choose Send reset link. The page always says to check your email, whether
or not that address has an account. Open the link in the email to choose a new
password of at least 8 characters, then sign in with it. A link that has
expired or was already used says so and lets you send a new one; you can ask
for another link after 60 seconds.

## Create an account
Page: /register
For: all
Keywords: sign up, register, create account, join, new account, role, tenant, landlord, provider

Choose your role when you register: Tenant, Service provider or Landlord.
After signing up, tenants and landlords start a short setup wizard and
providers start their onboarding; each saves your answers as you go, so you
can leave and finish later. TenantVoice staff accounts cannot be created by
signing up.

## Sign in problems
Page: /login
For: all
Keywords: sign in, log in, login, signed out, session expired, account suspended, deactivated, blocked

Sign in with your email and password. If your session expires, sign in
again; unsaved work stays in the tab. A forgotten password can be reset with
Forgot? on the sign-in page. A suspended,
deactivated or closed account cannot sign in, except that a suspended
provider can still sign in to finish jobs in progress. If you think your
account was blocked by mistake, contact support.

## Delete your account
Page: /contact
For: all
Keywords: delete account, close account, remove account, cancel account, deactivate, data

Account deletion is handled by the support team: use the Contact page to email
TenantVoice and include the email address of your account. The team explains
the next steps. Support answers within one business day, Monday to Friday.

## Contact the support team
Page: /contact
For: all
Keywords: contact, support, help, email, phone, call, human, person, staff, team

The Contact page has the support email and phone number. Email is best for
account, billing and listing questions, and the phone for urgent tenancy or
safety matters. The team is available Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm AEST, and
answers within one business day. The support chat cannot see your account or
change anything, so contact the team for anything about your own records.

## Notification settings
Page: /tenant/settings
For: tenant
Keywords: notifications, alerts, emails, switch off, turn off, mute, preferences, settings

Settings has a switch for each kind of notification: New messages,
Application updates, Service bookings, Reviews and Property matches. A kind
you switch off sends no new notifications. Account alerts, such as
verification decisions and security notices, always arrive. Notifications
appear in the app; TenantVoice does not send email or text reminders.

## Finish your tenant profile
Page: /tenant/profile-completion
For: tenant
Keywords: profile, completion, complete, score, percentage, onboarding, setup, finish setup, wizard

Profile Completion shows how complete your tenant profile is, with each
missing item and a link to fill it in. Your saved profile fills in the
application form when you apply, so a complete one saves typing. If you
skipped the setup wizard, your dashboard offers Finish setup to continue
where you stopped.

## Finish a landlord profile
Page: /owner/profile/completion
For: landlord
Keywords: profile, completion, complete, onboarding, setup, finish setup, wizard, business details

Your profile's completion page lists what is still missing from your
landlord setup, such as your business details or verification documents. If
you skipped the setup wizard, your dashboard offers Finish setup to continue
where you stopped.

## About this support chat
Page: /contact
For: all
Keywords: support chat, assistant, chatbot, bot, AI, limit, messages a day, can you, do it for me, human

The support chat answers questions about using TenantVoice from its help
pages and FAQs. It cannot see your account, listings, applications, bookings
or complaints, and it cannot change anything or act for you: it tells you
which page to use. It takes a limited number of messages a day and says when
you reach it. AI answers need a verified account; without one, or when AI is
unavailable, the chat shows the help pages that match your question. For
anything about your own records, contact the support team.
