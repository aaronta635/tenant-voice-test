# Network and messages

## Connect with landlords, tenants and providers
Page: /tenant/network
For: tenant
Keywords: connect, connection, request, network, add, follow, accept, decline, withdraw

Find people on Search Profiles, or open a member's public profile, and choose
Connect. Any active tenant, landlord or provider can ask any other member; you
cannot connect with yourself or with TenantVoice staff. Your Network page
lists Connection requests to accept or decline, your Sent requests (you can
withdraw one) and your Connections. Once a request is accepted, the two of you
can message each other. After someone declines your request, only they can
send a new one.

## Connect with other members as a landlord
Page: /owner/network
For: landlord
Keywords: connect, connection, request, network, add, follow, accept, decline, withdraw, tenants

Find tenants, landlords and providers on Search Profiles, or open a member's
public profile, and choose Connect. Each pair of members has one request at a
time, in either direction. Your Network page lists the requests you received
(Accept or Decline request), the ones you sent (Withdraw) and your
connections. Accepted connections can message each other. After someone
declines your request, only they can send a new one.

## Connect with clients as a provider
Page: /dashboard/network
For: service_provider
Keywords: connect, connection, request, network, clients, accept, decline, withdraw, recommendations

Your Network page lists the requests you received, the ones you sent and your
connections. Send a request with Connect from Search profiles, a member's
public profile, or the Recommendations page, which suggests landlords and
tenants in the areas you serve. Each pair of members has one request at a
time. Accepted connections can message each other, and after someone declines
your request, only they can send a new one.

## Message a landlord about a listing
Page: /tenant/messages
For: tenant
Keywords: message, contact, chat, landlord, owner, inspection, ask, phone, email

Contact details stay private on TenantVoice. Open a listing and choose Message
the landlord to start a conversation about it, then continue it on your
Messages page. You can also message any member you are connected with, and a
provider from their profile. To see a home, book one of the inspection times
on its listing, or message the landlord when none suits you.

## Messages for landlords
Page: /owner/messages
For: landlord
Keywords: message, contact, chat, tenant, applicant, inbox, reply

Your Messages page holds the conversations tenants start from your listings,
and those with your connections and with providers. Tenants never see your
phone number or email unless you share them in a message.

## Messages for providers
Page: /dashboard/messages
For: service_provider
Keywords: message, contact, chat, client, inbox, reply, enquiry

Clients message you from your public profile with Message on the booking card,
and your connections can message you too. Your Messages page holds every
conversation, including the threads of your bookings.

## Hide your profile from search
Page: /tenant/settings
For: tenant
Keywords: privacy, private, hide, search, public profile, visible, discoverable

In Settings, turn off Show my public profile to leave profile search: a hidden
profile does not appear in Search Profiles, and its public page is not found.
The same page has a switch for each kind of notification; account alerts
always arrive. Share my readiness score shows the average of the owner
scorecards you choose on your public profile; while it is off, none shows.

## Hide a landlord profile from search
Page: /owner/profile/settings
For: landlord
Keywords: privacy, private, hide, search, public profile, visible, notifications, settings

In your profile settings, turn off Show my public profile to leave profile
search: your public page is then not found either. The same page has a switch
for each kind of notification, such as New applications and Listing updates;
account alerts always arrive.
