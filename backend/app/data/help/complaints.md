# Complaints and repairs

## Lodge a complaint
Page: /tenant/complaints
For: tenant
Keywords: complaint, complain, report, problem, issue, landlord, unsafe, safety, harassment, lodge, raise, file

On the Complaints page choose Lodge a complaint. Pick the property it is
about, the category that fits best, a short subject and what happened
(include dates and what you have already tried), then choose Submit
complaint. A complaint is private and tracked to a resolution, unlike a public
review. The property's owner can see it and respond, and TenantVoice decides
its status. For an urgent safety matter, contact emergency services or NSW
Fair Trading directly.

## Follow a complaint
Page: /tenant/complaints
For: tenant
Keywords: complaint status, track, follow, open, under review, resolved, withdrawn, outcome, timeline, withdraw

Each complaint on your Complaints page shows its status (Open, Under review,
Resolved or Withdrawn), the owner's response and a timeline of every step,
including each status TenantVoice set and its note. When it is resolved, the
outcome appears on the complaint. You are notified when its status changes.
Until it is resolved you can choose Withdraw complaint. Complaints made about
you appear on the same page, where you can respond.

## Respond to a complaint about you or your property
Page: /owner/complaints
For: landlord
Keywords: complaint, respond, reply, response, against me, about me, tenant complaint, dispute

Complaints that tenants lodge about you or your properties appear on your
Complaints page, with filters for each status. Choose Respond to give your
side; you can update your response until the complaint is resolved. TenantVoice
decides each complaint and records every step on its timeline, which you and
the tenant both see.

## Respond to a complaint about your services
Page: /dashboard/complaints
For: service_provider
Keywords: complaint, respond, reply, response, against me, client complaint, dispute

Formal complaints about your services appear on your Complaints page. Choose
Respond to give your side; you can update it until the complaint is resolved.
TenantVoice decides each complaint, and its timeline shows every step to you
and to the person who lodged it.

## Ask for a repair
Page: /tenant/maintenance
For: tenant
Keywords: repair, maintenance, fix, broken, leak, fault, request, plumber, electrician

Repairs are separate from complaints. On Maintenance, pick the property and
describe what needs fixing, then choose Submit repair request. The owner sees
it on their Maintenance page. When a provider is assigned, they book a visit
and mark the job done, and you are notified at each step.

## Repair requests for owners
Page: /owner/maintenance
For: landlord
Keywords: repair, maintenance, fix, request, tenant repair, mark resolved, work order

Repair requests from your tenants appear on your Maintenance page; complaints
stay on the Complaints page. Choose Mark resolved once a repair is done. When
a provider is assigned to a repair, they book the visit and mark it done, and
you are notified at each step.

## Assigned repairs for providers
Page: /dashboard/repairs
For: service_provider
Keywords: repair, maintenance, job, assigned, visit, book visit, mark done, work order

Repairs assigned to you appear on Assigned repairs. Choose Book visit to set a
date, time and duration in Sydney time (Move visit changes it), and Mark done
with a note of what was done once the visit has started. The street address,
access notes and tenant's name show once you book the visit. Taking on repairs
needs a verified account.
