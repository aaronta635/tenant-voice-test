# Admin console

## Review verification documents
Page: /admin/verification
For: admin
Keywords: verification queue, approve, reject, documents, review, admin, decide, account verification

The Verification page groups waiting documents by account, oldest first, for
tenants, landlords and providers. Open a document through its 5-minute link,
then approve it or reject it with a reason. The account becomes verified once
every requirement of its role has an approved document, and the person is
notified of each decision. If another admin decided the same version first,
the page reloads that account instead. You cannot decide your own documents.

## Decide complaints
Page: /admin/complaints
For: admin
Keywords: complaints, decide, status, resolve, dismiss, assign, escalate, admin, case, categories, alert policies, alerts

The Complaints page lists every complaint with its category, parties and
status. Open one to assign it to an admin, read both sides and its timeline,
and set its status with a note that both parties see. The complaint forms
always offer the same fixed categories. Alert policies, under Complaints,
decide when a complaint raises an admin alert: each covers complaint
categories and words in a complaint's description, with a severity and a
response time, and the web and mobile apps share them. A policy you
deactivate stops raising alerts, and the complaints it covers keep their
categories.

## Moderate reviews and listings
Page: /admin/reviews
For: admin
Keywords: moderation, moderate, reported reviews, hide review, restore, listings, pause listing, archive listing

Reviews lists reported reviews: keep, hide or restore each one with a reason,
and the reports are marked decided. Listings lets you pause or archive a
listing with a reason. Each decision is recorded in the audit logs with you as
its actor, and the person it affects is notified.

## Suspend or restore an account
Page: /admin/users
For: admin
Keywords: suspend, deactivate, restore, enable, ban, block, account status, users, admin

Users lists every account. Open one to change its status with a reason, or to
decide its verification. A suspended provider can still sign in to finish
jobs in progress, but gets no new work, and their clients with upcoming
confirmed bookings are notified. A provider can be enabled again only while
their verification is approved.
