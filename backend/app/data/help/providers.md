# Provider workspace

## Set up and go live on the marketplace
Page: /dashboard/onboarding
For: service_provider
Keywords: go live, publish, marketplace, onboarding, setup, profile, listed, visible, appear in search

Provider onboarding has six steps: choose your category, add your services,
set your service area, verify your credentials, complete your public profile
(name, headline and bio), then Review & go live. Your answers save as you
go. Going live needs a verified account, so upload your credentials early.
Once live, your profile and its services appear in marketplace search and
clients can request bookings.

## Services and prices
Page: /dashboard/services
For: service_provider
Keywords: services, price, pricing, hourly, fixed, from price, add service, edit service, disable, inclusions

Add and edit what you offer on My services. Each service has a fixed price,
an hourly rate or a from price, shown to clients as $129, $85 per hour or
from $85, and can list what it includes and excludes. Turn a service off to
stop new bookings for it. A service that already has bookings cannot be
deleted, so it stays for those bookings; turn it off instead.

## Finances and invoices
Page: /dashboard/finances
For: service_provider
Keywords: finances, earnings, payments, paid, payout, invoice, fee, money, income, bank
Demo: frontend/app/(dashboard)/dashboard/finances/page.tsx

Finances shows a simulated ledger: confirming a booking issues an invoice,
and marking it done records a payment and the 8.5% platform fee. TenantVoice
does not charge clients, hold money or pay anything out, and payout records
are not bank transfers. Arrange payment with your client directly. The page
carries a Demo label for this reason.

## Reviews of your services
Page: /dashboard/reviews
For: service_provider
Keywords: reviews, rating, stars, reply, respond to review, report review, feedback

Clients review a booking once it is completed, one review per booking. Your
Reviews page lists them with filters for those you replied to and those
awaiting a reply; write a reply to answer one publicly. To report a review
that breaks the rules, use Report on it: a TenantVoice admin decides whether
to hide it, and until then it shows as reported.

## Recommendations and work opportunities
Page: /dashboard/recommendations
For: service_provider
Keywords: recommendations, suggestions, leads, opportunities, clients, landlords, tenants, find clients

Recommendations suggests landlords and tenants you might connect with, based
on their public profiles and the areas you serve. Describe who you want to
reach to the assistant, then choose Find matches. Work opportunities lists
owners and landlords with listings or a public area in your service areas,
with the facts behind each one. Nobody is contacted until you choose Connect
or Message.
