# Applications

## Apply for a property
Page: /tenant/properties
For: tenant
Keywords: apply, application, rent, lease, offer, submit, send, rental

Open a listing from Properties and choose Apply for this property. Your saved
profile fills in the form, and you can change anything before sending: the
move-in date, the people living there, the weekly rent you offer, your yearly
income (optional), pets, About you and a message to the landlord (optional).
Under Documents to share you can attach the documents you marked Also owners I
apply to. Choose Send application. Applying needs a verified account, and
only a listing that is open to applications takes them.

## Follow your applications
Page: /tenant/applications
For: tenant
Keywords: application status, track, follow, submitted, under review, shortlisted, approved, declined, withdrawn, expired

The Applications page lists every application with its status: Submitted,
Under review, Shortlisted, Approved, Declined, Withdrawn or Expired, and the
history of each step. When the landlord approves or declines, you get a
notification with their note if they wrote one. While the landlord has not
decided, you can choose Withdraw on the application. A declined, withdrawn or
expired application cannot be reopened, and each listing takes one
application from you.

## What the landlord sees in an application
Page: /tenant/applications
For: tenant
Keywords: privacy, landlord sees, owner sees, phone, email, contact details, documents, shared

The landlord and the listing's manager see your name, verified badge and
rating, and what you sent in the form. They never see your phone number or
email; they reply through TenantVoice. On the website, documents go with an
application only when you choose them in the form, and the owner can open them
while the application is open or approved, and while you still share them. In
the TenantVoice app, the owner of a listing you have an open or approved
application for can open all of your documents.

## Why you cannot apply yet
Page: /tenant/id-verification
For: tenant
Keywords: cannot apply, can't apply, apply button, verification needed, blocked, not allowed

Applying for rentals needs a verified account: upload your passport, driver
licence and Medicare card on the ID Verification page and wait for an admin to
approve all three. If the listing says the
landlord isn't taking new applications, it is closed to applications for now.
If you already applied, the listing shows your application's status and a link
to your applications instead. Applications are made from a tenant account.

## Book an inspection and sign your lease
Page: /tenant/inspections
For: tenant
Keywords: inspection, inspection time, book inspection, viewing, open home, lease, sign lease, confirm lease, move in, rent, my rent

Book one of the inspection times a landlord published, from the listing page
or your rental pipeline. The landlord accepts it, declines it or asks you to
pick another time; your Inspections page lists them, with any meeting link.
Once your application is approved, your landlord sends the lease: open it on
your Leases page, confirm its terms and sign it with your typed name. The
landlord signs it next and activates it. My rent shows the rent charges and
the payments your landlord records. TenantVoice does not collect rent.
