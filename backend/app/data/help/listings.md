# Listings

## List a property
Page: /owner/properties/create
For: landlord
Keywords: list, listing, add property, new listing, advertise, create, publish, rent out

Go to Properties and choose Add Property. The wizard has three steps:
Property Details (title, address, suburb, state, postcode, type, bedrooms and
bathrooms), Photos & Description, and Rental Terms (weekly rent, bond, lease
term and the date it is available from). Choose Publish Listing on the last
step to put it on the public listings, where tenants can find it and apply.
Publishing needs a verified account. Until you are verified, choose Save it
unpublished: the listing stays off the market and you can publish it from its
page later.

## Edit a listing and its photos
Page: /owner/properties
For: landlord
Keywords: edit, change, update, photos, pictures, images, description, rent, price, details

Open the listing from Properties and choose Edit listing (or Edit on its
card). A save changes only the fields you change; you can update the
description, rent, bond, lease term,
rooms, features and photos at any time, also while the listing is published.
The title, address and rent can be changed but not left empty. A photo you
remove is deleted from TenantVoice.

## Listing statuses
Page: /owner/properties
For: landlord
Keywords: status, pause, close applications, leased, relist, reopen, delete, archive, off market, draft

Each listing's page offers the moves its status allows. Publish listing puts a
draft on the public listings. Close applications keeps a published listing
public but stops new applications, and Reopen applications starts them again;
a listing closed to applications cannot be paused. Pause listing takes a
published listing off the public listings while its applications stay, and
Publish again puts it back. A listing is leased only when you approve one of
its applications, which also declines its other open applications. A leased
listing's status no longer changes: to let the home again, create a new
listing. Delete listing removes a listing that is not leased, with its photos
and applications, and its page for good. Moving a listing back to published
needs a verified account.

## Review the applications for a listing
Page: /owner/applications
For: landlord
Keywords: applications, applicants, review, shortlist, approve, decline, accept, reject, note, tenant documents

Applications appear under their listing and on the Applications page. Each
shows the applicant's name, verified badge and rating and what they chose to
send, never their phone number or email. Choose Shortlist to mark one, or
Approve or Decline to decide it; the applicant is told of your decision, with
your note if you add one. Documents the tenant shared open from the
application while it is open or approved. A declined, withdrawn or expired
application cannot be decided again.

## Listings that tenants see
Page: /properties
For: tenant, landlord
Keywords: browse, search, find, properties, homes, rentals, listings, address, location

The public listings show every published listing and those closed to new
applications. Signed out, a listing shows only its suburb, state and postcode;
signed-in members also see the street. You can search by location, and open a
listing to see its photos, rent, rooms, reviews and the landlord's public
profile. Contact details are never shown: tenants message the landlord through
TenantVoice.

## Inspection times, leases and the rent ledger
Page: /owner/inspections
For: landlord
Keywords: inspection, inspection time, open home, viewing, lease, draft lease, send lease, sign lease, activate lease, rent ledger, receipt, expense

Publish inspection times on a property's Inspections tab, then accept or
decline each request, or ask the tenant to pick another time. You can also
schedule a private, remote or routine inspection with an applicant. For an
approved application, prepare the lease on your Leases page, preview its PDF
and send it; once the tenant confirms and signs it, you sign it and activate
it. Your Rent ledger lists the rent charges of your active tenancies, the
receipts you record and your expenses, with a CSV export. TenantVoice does
not collect rent.
