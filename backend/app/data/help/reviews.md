# Reviews

## Write a review of a rental
Page: /tenant/write-review
For: tenant
Keywords: review, rate, rating, stars, write review, feedback, property review, landlord review, review photos

You can review a home once your application for it is approved, one review
per home. Write a Review has three steps: the home and eight ratings from 1
to 5 (condition at move-in, the owner's responsiveness, fairness, value,
maintenance, repair quality, safety and privacy, and the neighbourhood);
what happened, such as problems, whether they were disclosed and whether you
would recommend it, with an optional answer about leaving after you asked for
repairs; then a title, your review, pros, cons, advice to the owner and up to
4 photos, and two confirmations. On the website, a review publishes on the
listing as a "Verified tenant", without your name, and only you and
TenantVoice admins read the answer about leaving. In the TenantVoice app,
signed-in members, the home's owner included, can see your review with your
name and that answer. Signed-in members see the photos, and a photo's link
carries your account's ID, so a member who opens one could find your public
profile.

## Reviews of your property
Page: /owner/properties
For: landlord
Keywords: review, reviews, rating, reply, respond to review, report review, property review

Tenants with an approved application can review your property, once each.
On the website, reviews show on the listing's page, naming the reviewer only
as Verified tenant or Tenant. In the TenantVoice app, signed-in members, you
included, can see each review with its reviewer's name and their answer about
leaving after asking for repairs. Replying to a property review is not
available on the website yet. To report one that breaks the rules, choose
Report under it on the listing's page.

## How ratings and reviews are shown
Page: /properties
For: all
Keywords: rating, ratings, stars, average, review count, reviewer name, anonymous, reported, hidden

A member's rating is the average of the published reviews about them,
reported ones included. On the website, public reviews never name the
reviewer or show their photo: a property review says Verified tenant after a
confirmed tenancy, or Tenant. In the TenantVoice app, signed-in members see a
property review with its reviewer's name. A review someone reported stays
listed, marked as reported, until a TenantVoice admin keeps or hides it.

## Ask an owner for a scorecard
Page: /tenant/dashboard#scorecards
For: tenant
Keywords: scorecard, tenant scorecard, reference, owner reference, rate me, readiness score, share score, public profile

You can ask the owner or agent of a home you rent or rented through
TenantVoice, a lease with them or an application they approved, to complete
your tenant scorecard: choose Request scorecard on the Scorecards card of your
dashboard. They rate seven items from 1 to 5, with an optional comment, or
decline, and you are told either way. Each owner completes one scorecard per
home. After a decline you can ask them again 30 days later, and after you
cancel a request, 7 days later. You choose which completed scorecards show on
your public profile, as an average with no names or comments, and none shows
while Share my readiness score is off in Settings.

## Scorecard requests from tenants
Page: /owner/scorecard
For: landlord
Keywords: scorecard, tenant scorecard, reference, rate tenant, scorecard request, decline, rating

A tenant whose application you approved, or whose lease with you started, can
ask you for a tenant scorecard. Open Scorecards in your menu to rate the seven
items from 1 to 5, with an optional comment, or to decline with a reason; the
tenant is told. The tenant decides whether it shows on their public profile.
A request arrives as a review notice, so it follows the Reviews switch in your
settings, and Scorecards lists every request either way.
