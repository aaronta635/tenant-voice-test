# Verification

## How tenants get verified
Page: /tenant/id-verification
For: tenant
Keywords: verify, verified, identity, ID, passport, licence, license, Medicare, Medicare card, documents, badge

A tenant account needs three approved documents to be verified: a passport,
a driver licence (front and back in one file) and a Medicare card. Upload each
on the ID Verification page: choose Upload on that document's card and pick a
PDF, JPEG, PNG or WebP file of up to 5 MB. Each document shows as in review
until a TenantVoice admin decides it, and you get a notification with each
decision. Once all three are approved your profile shows the verified badge.
Other documents, such as a government photo ID card, do not count towards it.
You need a verified account to apply for rentals and to get AI answers.

## How landlords and agents get verified
Page: /owner/profile/verification
For: landlord
Keywords: verify, verified, identity, ID, passport, licence, ownership, rates notice, title, management authority, agency agreement, agent, documents, badge

A landlord account needs two approved documents to be verified: a government
photo ID (a passport, a driver licence or another government photo ID card,
uploaded as Government photo ID) and proof of ownership (a rates notice, a
title search or a contract of sale in your name). Upload both on the
Verification documents page, under your profile. A management authority can
be kept there too, but it does not count towards verification. A TenantVoice
admin reviews each document and you are notified of every decision.
Publishing a listing needs a verified account. An agent account has no
documents to send for verification: TenantVoice reviews the documents of
tenants, landlords and providers only.

## How service providers get verified
Page: /dashboard/profile#verification-documents
For: service_provider
Keywords: verify, verified, credentials, ID, insurance, public liability, certificate of currency, trade licence, licence, license, ABN, badge

A provider account needs three approved documents to be verified, whatever
your category: a government photo ID, a trade licence and public liability
insurance (a certificate of currency). Add them in the Verification documents
section of your profile, where they are Government ID, Professional licence and
Insurance, each with Upload file. An ABN registration can be added there too,
but it does not count towards verification. The trust team reviews each one.
Until your account is verified you cannot go live on the marketplace, accept
or start bookings, or take on repairs.

## When a document is rejected or replaced
Page: /tenant/id-verification
For: tenant
Keywords: rejected, declined, replace, reupload, reason, pending, review, status

A rejected document shows the reviewer's reason on its card. Choose Replace
and upload a new file to send it for review again. Replacing or deleting a
document that was approved sends it back to review, so your account returns to
pending until it is approved again, unless another approved document still
meets that requirement. Documents verification does not ask for, such as a
bank statement, never change whether you are verified.

## When a landlord's document is rejected or replaced
Page: /owner/profile/verification
For: landlord
Keywords: rejected, declined, replace, reupload, reason, pending, review, status

A rejected document shows the reviewer's reason on its card. Choose Replace
and upload a new file to send it for review again. Replacing or deleting an
approved document sends it back to review, so your account is pending again
until it is approved, unless another approved document still meets that
requirement. While you are pending, published listings stay as they are, but
you cannot publish a new one.

## When a provider's credential is rejected or replaced
Page: /dashboard/profile#verification-documents
For: service_provider
Keywords: rejected, declined, replace, reupload, reason, pending, review, status, credential

A rejected credential shows the reviewer's reason in its row. Choose Replace
file to send a new one for review. Replacing a verified credential removes its
badge until the trust team approves the new file, and your account is pending
until every credential it needs is approved again.

## Who can see your documents
Page: /tenant/id-verification
For: tenant
Keywords: privacy, private, safe, share, sharing, owners, who can see, visibility, documents, passport, ID

Documents never appear on your public profile. You and TenantVoice admins can
open them, each time through a link that works for 5 minutes. On the website,
Who can see it on a document's card lets you choose Also owners I apply to:
such a document goes with an application only when you pick it in the
application form, and the listing's owner can open it while that application
is open or approved. In the TenantVoice app, the owner of a listing you have an
open or approved application for can open all of your documents, whatever you
choose on the website.
