# Service bookings

## Book a service provider
Page: /categories
For: tenant, landlord, service_provider
Keywords: book, booking, hire, provider, tradie, cleaner, plumber, service, request, find providers

Choose Find Providers, pick a category and open a provider's profile. Under a
service choose Book this service, then on the Request a booking card choose the
service, an available date and time (Sydney time) and the property suburb, and
choose Request to book. This sends a request for the provider to accept; no
charge is made. Choose Message on the same card to ask the provider a
question first.

## Follow and cancel your bookings
Page: /tenant/bookings
For: tenant
Keywords: booking status, cancel, reschedule, change time, new enquiry, confirmed, in progress, completed, declined, cancelled

Service bookings lists every request you made with its status: New enquiry,
Confirmed, In progress, Completed, Declined or Cancelled. You can choose
Cancel booking while it is a new enquiry or confirmed. Once the provider has
started the job, message them to stop it. To change a confirmed time, ask the
provider: they can move the booking.

## Follow and cancel an owner's bookings
Page: /owner/bookings
For: landlord
Keywords: booking status, cancel, reschedule, change time, new enquiry, confirmed, in progress, completed, declined, cancelled

Service bookings lists the requests you made to providers with their status:
New enquiry, Confirmed, In progress, Completed, Declined or Cancelled. You can
choose Cancel booking while it is a new enquiry or confirmed; once the job has
started, message the provider. To change a confirmed time, ask the provider:
they can move the booking.

## Manage the bookings clients make with you
Page: /dashboard/bookings
For: service_provider
Keywords: accept, decline, confirm, start job, mark done, complete, reschedule, enquiry, bookings

New requests arrive as New enquiry on your Bookings page. Choose Accept
booking or Decline booking. On a confirmed booking choose Start job once its
time has come, and Mark done when the work is finished; you can move a new
enquiry or a confirmed booking to another time. Accepting and starting jobs
need a verified account; a suspended account can still finish a job in
progress. Bookings you make with other providers are on My booking requests.

## Availability
Page: /dashboard/availability
For: service_provider
Keywords: availability, hours, opening hours, calendar, times, days off, schedule

Set your Weekly hours on the Availability page, and an availability note for
clients if you like. Clients can only request free times within those hours,
in Sydney time and up to two weeks ahead; a time another booking takes is not
offered.
